# Platform Features

TwinPix Workspace is composed of several deeply integrated modules designed to handle the entire lifecycle of influencer marketing.

## Influencer CRM
A comprehensive database of creators. Allows teams to filter by engagement rate, category, and followers. Includes custom tags, negotiation terms, and tracking of outreach stages (Lead, Contacted, Active).

## Campaign Management
End-to-end management of marketing campaigns. Link campaigns to specific clients, set budgets, define overall deliverables, and assign specific influencers to the roster.

## Projects
Organize multiple campaigns under master projects for larger enterprise clients. Provides high-level visibility into overall client health and progress.

## Tasks
A robust task engine. Features include:
- Status tracking (Todo, In Progress, Review, Done)
- Priority levels
- Subtask checklists
- Due dates and recurring rules
- Time tracking estimation vs. actuals

## Calendar
Unified scheduling view. Track campaign launches, internal meetings, content review deadlines, and specific deliverable due dates across the agency.

## Notifications
Multi-channel alerts keeping the team aligned.
- **In-App**: Real-time bell notifications.
- **Email**: Daily digests and urgent alerts.
- **WhatsApp**: Direct messaging for critical updates.

## AI Assistant
Integrated ChatGPT capabilities that power the workspace:
- **Creator Insights**: Automatically generate strengths/weaknesses profiles for influencers.
- **Brand Match Analysis**: Score an influencer's fit for a specific campaign.
- **Copilot**: Conversational interface for querying workspace data.

## Analytics
Dashboard visualizing key performance indicators (KPIs) for both internal team velocity and external influencer campaign performance.

## Activity Timeline
Granular history tracking. Every status change, file upload, or comment is logged chronologically on the respective Task, Campaign, or Client page.

## Audit Logs
Enterprise-grade security logging. Tracks destructive actions, permission changes, and data exports by admins for compliance.

## Search
Global search powered by `fuse.js` allows rapid navigation to specific clients, tasks, or influencers from any screen.

## Files
Centralized asset management. Store and categorize brand assets, signed contracts, invoices, and draft content videos linked directly to their respective tasks or campaigns.

## Exports
One-click generation of PDF and CSV reports for client-facing performance reviews or internal auditing.
